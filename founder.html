The Foundation

Before founding ApexOps, Jay worked across the full Revenue Operations stack — architecting Zoho and HubSpot CRM environments, engineering automated workflows in Zapier and Make, and building Power BI dashboards that replaced manual reporting entirely.

He has operated inside organisations where data silos, disconnected pipelines, and blind strategic decisions weren't abstract consulting problems — they were daily operational realities costing real time and real revenue. That experience is the foundation of every engagement ApexOps takes on.

The Approach

ApexOps was built on a single conviction: that most companies don't need more strategy — they need working systems. Every engagement Jay leads is scoped around a defined business outcome, built with precision, documented in full, and handed over to a team that can maintain it independently.

No retainer dependency. No black-box configurations your team can't understand. No vague deliverables measured in hours rather than outcomes.

Today

Jay works with a focused roster of clients — logistics companies, B2B SaaS firms, and professional services groups — who are past the point where manual operations are acceptable and ready to invest in the infrastructure that makes their next stage of growth structurally sustainable.

Each engagement is taken on personally. There is no team of junior consultants doing the work while a senior partner handles check-ins. When you engage ApexOps, you work directly with Jay from scoping call to final handoff.
